01. About Me

I'm Paul Specht, an MSBA candidate at Wake Forest University School of Business with a background in Mechanical Engineering and minors in Math and Physics. Originally from Germany, I came to Wake Forest to compete as a Division I cross country and track athlete, an experience that first sparked my interest in data.

Translating my own training and performance data into decisions that actually moved the needle showed me how powerful structured data can be. That same instinct now drives my work in analytics: I'm drawn to problems where data can be turned into clear, actionable insight, whether that's modeling player value in professional sports or refining a medical device prototype.

I'm currently exploring opportunities in the sports and automotive industries, where I can combine technical rigor with a genuine curiosity for performance.
