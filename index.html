04. My Work & Projects

MSBA Summer Project

Soccer Player Valuation Analysis

As part of an team project, our group set out to understand what actually drives a professional soccer player's market value. Using a dataset of professional football players from the FIFA World Cup, we applied regression and correlation analysis in Python (pandas, matplotlib) and Excel to clean, explore, and model the relationships between player attributes and valuation. The goal was to move past intuition and surface the quantitative factors that clubs and analysts could use to make more informed decisions on player recruitment and investment

My Role: Data Cleaning, Regression Modeling & Data Visualization

Outcome: Identified key drivers of player valuation, including position, league tier, age & assists

Engineeering Capstone Project

Heart Preservation Device

Donor hearts for transplant are currently transported on ice, limiting viability to just 4 hours and restricting how far hearts can travel, which reduces transplant access for underserved and rural populations. Our capstone team designed a heart transport device that integrates hypothermic oxygenated perfusion, temperature regulation, and data monitoring to safely extend preservation time than existing solutions on the market.

My Role: Mechanical Lead: Research, CAD Modeling, Built the Perfusion Circuit & Ventilation System

Outcome: We built and tested a working prototype, complete with a custom aortic cannula and closed perfusion circuit, validated on porcine hearts, extending viability time from 3 to 4 hours
